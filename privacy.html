Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy describes how the Beverly website handles information when you visit it. The site does not require an account.

Analytics

The Beverly homepage uses Google Analytics to understand site usage. When you visit that page, Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, interactions, browser and device details, and approximate location. It may use cookies or similar technologies. Google processes this information to provide analytics services under its own terms and privacy policy.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and learn about Google’s available controls in Google Analytics opt-out options.

Browser storage

The homepage stores a temporary setting in your browser’s sessionStorage to remember if you dismissed the Beverly Agent notice during the current browser session. This setting is not sent to Beverly and is cleared when the browser session ends.

Contacting us

If you email victor@beverlyui.com, the information you include will be handled through your email provider and the recipient’s email service so they can respond. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

Third-party links

The site links to services such as GitHub and crates.io. When you follow those links, those services may collect information under their own privacy policies. This policy does not cover third-party services.

Updates and questions

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The date above indicates when it was last revised. For questions about this policy, contact victor@beverlyui.com.
